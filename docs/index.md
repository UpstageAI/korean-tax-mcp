# Korean Tax MCP — Korean tax law for AI agents

Free, open-source MCP servers that let AI assistants answer Korean tax questions with the statute behind every answer.

- **[Use cases for foreign companies, expats and Korean subsidiaries](use-cases/)** — royalty withholding to a US parent, 183-day residency, expat cost recharges, VAT reverse charge, the 2026 100% exclusion for foreign subsidiary dividends
- **Korean tax law MCP** (statutes as in force on any date, NTS rulings, court cases, 96 tax treaties, residency and PE tests): [GitHub](https://github.com/UpstageAI/korean-tax-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/korean-tax-mcp/) · [llms.txt](https://github.com/UpstageAI/korean-tax-mcp/blob/main/llms.txt)
- **Korean tax calculator MCP** (corporate, income, VAT, withholding, foreign worker flat tax, transfer pricing and more, with statute citations): [GitHub](https://github.com/UpstageAI/korean-tax-calc-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/korean-tax-calc-mcp/)
- **Korean government document MCP** (official reports as HWPX): [GitHub](https://github.com/UpstageAI/korean-report-hwpx)

Install (Claude Desktop / Claude Code): `uvx korean-tax-mcp` and `uvx korean-tax-calc-mcp`.

*Not tax advice — verify against the statute and with a qualified professional.*
